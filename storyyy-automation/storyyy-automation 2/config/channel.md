# Storyyy Timee - channel voice (EDIT THIS FILE - it is fed into every story)

Narrator: a warm, calm storyteller speaking to ONE listener, like a friend at a late-night kitchen table.
Tone: hopeful but honest. Never preachy. The lesson is felt, not announced.
Stories: short, original, set in ordinary places (a bus stop, a night shift, a small shop, a village school).
Characters: have real names and one odd specific detail (a cracked blue mug, a limp from an old injury).
Language: simple English that is easy to follow for listeners who are not native speakers.
Ending: a quiet turn or a single memorable closing line. No "like and subscribe" in the story.

Never: copy famous speeches or quotes, invent fake statistics, give medical or money advice,
use generic "believe in yourself" language, or open with a greeting.

Series ideas to rotate (add your own): "The Night Shift Files", "Letters I Never Sent", "Small Town Lessons".
